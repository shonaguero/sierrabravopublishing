Debut Album
Old Haunts
"Then Again" — Out August 28

The first batch of songs built from the long road between home and wherever the highway takes you next. In his early twenties, Shon ran a small recording studio, made records for other artists, and played in bands across Oklahoma and Texas. Between 1999 and 2004 he wrote albums-worth of songs, then packed the demos away and put the guitar down. More than twenty years of a life well lived went by. Then he opened a box and found the songs he had left behind. Turns out he finally understood what he had been writing about.

Presave "Then Again"
"Songs from the space between where you came from and where you ended up."
